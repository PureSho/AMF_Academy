The AMF standard

Good players keep learning. Great teammates help others grow.

  • Respect teammates, opponents, coaches, and community members.
  • Accept constructive feedback and give it respectfully.
  • Take responsibility for your actions.
  • Measure progress by effort, consistency, and teamwork—not only wins.
