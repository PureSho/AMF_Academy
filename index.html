What we're about

More than mechanics.
It's how you grow.

Academy is designed to help players improve over time—not just chase a win. The goal is to develop game sense, consistency, communication, and the habits that make a strong teammate.

↗

Improve with purpose

Focus on practical skills, learn from mistakes, and turn each session into a chance to get better.

⌁

Play as a team

Build communication, awareness, and trust so teammates can make better decisions together.

✦

Keep competition healthy

Take competition seriously without taking it out on people. Respect is part of the standard.

The academy approach

Train smart.
Stay coachable.

Every player starts somewhere. Academy is about showing up ready to learn, listening to feedback, and putting in consistent effort. Training activities and expectations may vary by game, coach, and current program.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions.

What is AMF Academy?

AMF Academy is a learning-focused program within the AMF community intended to help players develop skills, teamwork, and game knowledge.

Do I need to be an experienced player?

No specific experience level is promised on this page. Reach out to the AMF team to learn about current entry requirements and which opportunities are available.

Which games does Academy support?

Supported games and training schedules can change. Check the official AMF community for the current program list and schedule.

How do I join?

Use the official AMF community invite or contact method. This website's Join button needs to be connected to the current official link before launch.

What does AMF stand for?

AMF stands for “Always Moving Forward”—a reminder to keep learning, stay respectful, and improve together.
